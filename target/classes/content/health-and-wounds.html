Health affects movement and access to after-hours activities; some substances create addictions.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
WoundsScavenging can cause wounds that block or disrupt activity.
MedicineBandages, injectors and other medical supplies treat different conditions.
AddictionsWithdrawal can affect negotiations with sellers and buyers.
SynaprestIn Officer Lun’s story, each injector at day 16 reduces his addiction score by 10.

Practical checklist

  • Treat health problems before dangerous scavenging trips.
  • Monitor addiction-related price penalties.
  • Keep emergency medicine separate from quest supplies.
