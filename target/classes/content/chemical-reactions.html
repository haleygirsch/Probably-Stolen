This page covers customer complaints and repeat visits caused by chemical products, not the laboratory reaction recipes. A buyer using a flawed pill, drink, vial or injector may return to complain.

Reaction system

TopicDetails
ChanceEach reaction level adds an 8% chance of a complaint.
ExampleLevel 1 = 8%, level 3 = 24%, level 5 = 40%.
Product severityLethal +5 levels, toxic +4; false product labels and incorrect effects can add more.
Who can reactCustomers using the product personally; resellers, tourists and one-off visitors are excluded.
OutcomeA written complaint or face-to-face return can cost Credits and faction reputation.
Follow-upA product without flaws can instead cause a happy customer to return and buy more.

Prevent negative reactions

  • Use the Composition Spectrometer to check what is actually in a liquid before selling it.
  • Do not sell mislabeled products or claim a grade higher than the tested purity.
  • The Chemistry tab contains the separate mixing and temperature mechanics.
