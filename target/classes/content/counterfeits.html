Other goodsSome injectors and premium items can also be fraudulent.
Inspection resultA correct confrontation can trigger a -15% buying discount.

Practical checklist

  • Keep identification guides near your inspection workflow.
  • Inspect suspicious versions before making a high-value purchase.
  • Do not classify something counterfeit by appearance alone without the game’s confirmation.

Spotting and confronting fake products

Additional game-reference details, rewritten and organized for quick lookup.

MechanicHow it works
Affected goodsHemingway cigarettes, Food Stamps and Immunivax Injectors.
InspectionUse the Magnifying Glass and look at the specific warning marks in the tray.
Accusing correctlyCan reset the item to its actual value and add a 15% inspection discount.
Accusing incorrectlyCosts 2 reputation with the seller’s faction.
Confirming a genuine itemFor supported stamp/cigarette interactions, correctly defending authenticity can gain 2 reputation.
Selling fake itemsBeing caught can cause a 4-point faction reputation loss.
Hemingway clueSecurity seal, spelling, print color, font and manufacturer mark should all be checked.

Playing tips

  • Only the highest-index issue ticked in a multi-select confrontation is evaluated, not every checkmark.
  • Injector variants have different confrontation limitations.
