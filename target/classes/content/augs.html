Five-step identification process

  1. Screen: pay extra attention to requests for medical supplies or materials. This only identifies candidates, not proof.
  2. Look: check the face for a visible Aug chip, then assess posture and unusual outfits.
  3. Listen: notice unnaturally mechanical dialogue or awkward phrasing.
  4. Test: cautiously test a small price change when visual and dialogue clues are ambiguous.
  5. Verify: if you remain unsure, use an Aug Scanner. Avoid a wrong accusation based only on one clue.
Do not assume

Asking for materials alone is not enough to identify an Aug. The game has false positives. Scanner results are useful for uncertainty.
