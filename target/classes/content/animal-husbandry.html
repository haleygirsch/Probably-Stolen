Rats are the breedable land animals. Manage age, hunger, cage capacity and harvest weight.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
Adult age5 days old.
Pregnancy3 nights.
Base litter3 offspring.
Elder11 days.
Old age14 days, unless Longevity genes extend productive growth.
MachinesAnimal Cage, Livestock Feed Dispenser, Auto Butcher and Cleaner Robot.

Practical checklist

  • Maintain feed; hunger below 400 slows growth.
  • Protect breeding animals from the Auto Butcher.
  • Use genes if pursuing longer lifespan or better size.

GestationPregnancy takes three nights.
Litter sizeThree pups is the baseline litter.
Hunger impactBelow 400 hunger, growth can slow significantly; starvation halves progress.
Equipment chainAnimal Cage → Livestock Feed Dispenser → optional Auto Butcher and Cleaner Robot.

Playing tips

  • Use the Marking Pen for an animal that you do not want the Auto Butcher to harvest.
  • The Retired Rat Rancher is the source for rats and specialized equipment.
