Luck is a numerical roll modifier affecting scavenging, crates, lottery and inspections.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
Start0 by default.
Lucky / Unlucky+15 / -15 from starting perks.
Gambler’s Coin+5 while carried.
Liquid Luck+75 for 3 days, given and later sold by Georgio.
Cap95 is the highest effective Luck.

Practical checklist

  • Use temporary Liquid Luck before opening many chance-based crates.
  • Lottery and scratcher behavior tracks Luck at creation as well as opening in some cases.
  • Keep a coin if you want a passive boost.

InspectionsLuck can sometimes cause inspectors to overlook illegal goods.

Playing tips

  • A successful Luck roll is not a guarantee of positive loot from every event.
  • Save Liquid Luck for periods when several Luck-dependent actions occur close together.
