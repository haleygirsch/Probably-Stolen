The display case is used to attract shoppers for particular kinds of goods.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
Visible stockItems placed for sale influence which customers approach.
Neon signsWater, Food, Household, Material, Firearm, Medical and Heavy Firearm signs emphasize a stock category.
VarietySome customers buy only one of each item and earn a Variety Buyer Bonus.

Practical checklist

  • Display only the goods you actually want to sell.
  • Swap specialist inventory as your production changes.
  • Do not inadvertently put quest containers in front of risky visitors.
