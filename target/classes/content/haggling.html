Each side of the transaction gets one bargain attempt, plus separate Blackmail and Threaten opportunities.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
Bargain steps10%, 25%, 50% and 75% are available offers.
Offer DiscountAlways accepted when the customer buys; budget increases by twice the discount percentage.
Add MarkupRolled acceptance; the buyer’s budget shrinks if accepted.
Demand DiscountA rolled discount on goods the customer is selling.
Blackmail / ThreatenPotential 20% better prices, with additional risks.
UnlockMentor explains on day 8, or available from start with Skip Intro.

Practical checklist

  • Use discounts when a customer cannot afford the displayed stock.
  • Only gamble on aggressive markups when losing the customer is acceptable.
  • Dr. Jackson and some other vendors do not haggle.

Bargain actions and consequences

Additional game-reference details, rewritten and organized for quick lookup.

MechanicHow it works
Bargain levels10%, 25%, 50% and 75% are the available change sizes.
Selling: discountAlways accepted; customer budget increases by twice the selected discount percentage.
Selling: markupChance-based; acceptance raises your sale price but reduces the customer’s available budget.
Buying: offer moreAlways accepted; you voluntarily pay extra.
Buying: request lessChance-based; successful negotiation reduces your purchase cost.
Attempt limitOne bargain for the buying side and one for the selling side of a customer interaction.
Other tacticsBlackmail and Threaten can improve the price by 20% but have risk and distinct usage rules.
Reputation riskA customer walking out after a failed interaction can reduce faction reputation by 3.

Playing tips

  • The Mentor introduces bargaining in the first story week; Skip Intro makes it available immediately.
  • Dr. Jackson and certain special visitors do not negotiate.
