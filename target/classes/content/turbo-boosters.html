Turbo boosters cause one immediate extra machine cycle when charged and used with valid inputs.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
DisposableOne extra cycle; consumed on use, base value 35 Credits.
AdvancedReusable. Recharges 5 per night; 15 charge is needed for a boost. Base value 300 Credits.
HypertechBanks extra charge; each 15 provides a boost. Base value 15,000 Credits.
CompatibilityWorks on multiple powered machines including Water Purifier, 3D Printer, Furnace, Hydroponics and Evaporator.

Practical checklist

  • Prepare inputs and power before triggering the extra cycle.
  • Do not buy a booster before your production chain is ready.
  • Check the wiki’s compatibility list for the target machine.

One-cycle boosters

Additional game-reference details, rewritten and organized for quick lookup.

MechanicHow it works
Disposable Turbo Booster35 Credits; immediately triggers one extra cycle and is consumed.
Advanced Turbo Booster300 Credits; rechargeable; draws 5 kJ to gain 5 charge nightly; each boost costs 15 charge.
Hypertech Turbo Booster15,000 Credits; gains 5 charge each cycle and can bank additional charge.
Hypertech stackingInstalling an Advanced Booster increases its charge generation and its power demand.
Machines supportedIncludes Hydroponic System, Furnace, Water Purifier, 3D Printer, DNA Manipulator, AgeWell and others.
Required inputBoosters do not produce an output unless the machine has valid ingredients or input stock.

Playing tips

  • Use boosts on expensive, ready-to-process batches rather than machines with empty input slots.
  • A reusable Advanced Booster needs a battery of its own.
