Hydroponic systems require water, suitable mineral concentrations and power. The Plant Analyzer reveals the growth score, which starts at 100% and falls when conditions are poor.

Grow a huge Nutrifruit

  1. Prepare a Hydroponic System with a working energy cell.
  2. Use AquaScan to test the water’s mineral and contaminant levels.
  3. Aim for roughly 0.50%–2.50% minerals and keep heavy metals, organic waste, microbes and chemical contaminants under the crop’s tolerated limits. Do not rely on the overall water grade alone.
  4. Plant Nutrifruit and check the Plant Analyzer as it grows. Aim for a final growth score of 96% or higher.
  5. Dilute water if its mineral ratio is too high. The creator found that an apparently acceptable displayed 2.46% mineral result could calculate just above 2.5% once divided by water content.

Starter hydroponics loop

Use Fillerweed as an inexpensive early crop. Grow Hydroreed in a secondary system to clean water each night, then transfer improved water to the crop-growing system. Cotton/Kotton becomes a stronger money crop once water and minerals are reliable.

Monitoring routine

  • Check battery and water before sleeping.
  • Scan the actual mixture, not just its overall quality label.
  • Use Nutrient Tablets when mineral levels are too low.
  • Keep a separate clean-water batch for high-value crops.
