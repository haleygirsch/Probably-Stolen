Rent is collected on a weekly schedule, and a loan or mortgage may bridge the purchase price.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
LandlordAppears on Sundays to collect rent.
MortgageLoan Shark offers a purchase option once you can meet the required down payment.
Trade BoardAn official comes Wednesdays from day 38 while you do not own a Business Permit.
Shop ownershipPurchasing the store is a primary win condition and may unlock Joe Wild’s Hypertech referral.

Practical checklist

  • Always keep upcoming rent funds separate.
  • Compare the cost of a new machine against any mortgage due soon.
  • Use Storylines to avoid accidentally triggering Lou before you are prepared.

Playing tips

  • Keep rent funds separate from profit you plan to reinvest in equipment.
  • The property cost begins increasing after day 50, so purchasing early can be more economical.
