Food provides calories; drinks can have quality bonuses, and special items affect Luck or health.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
FoodDifferent meals and fillets have different calorie totals.
WaterA drinkable water grade changes sale price; see Water grades.
FridgeChilled water or drinks get +20% value; frozen or ice-cold cases can gain +25%.
Liquid Luck+75 Luck for three days when consumed.
RescueSerpico’s storyline requires delivery of 2,000 kcal, not 2,000 Credits.

Practical checklist

  • Track calories and quantities before a rescue delivery.
  • Use fresh food in the fridge if its item type is supported.
  • Read labels before treating a liquid as drinking water.
