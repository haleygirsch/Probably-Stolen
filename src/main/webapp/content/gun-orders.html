A gun order pays for the requirements your build meets, not merely for the number of attachments installed.

Order workflow

  1. Place a Weapon Permit in your display case to attract buyers.
  2. Read the requested minimum weapon stats and the separate bonus conditions.
  3. Choose an affordable base weapon and fit only attachments that turn useful order requirements green.
  4. Confirm the payout, complete the order, then offer suitable ammo or melee goods when requested.
Different request types

Some customers request a specific build. Others say “give me your best” or bring their own weapon for modification. The best purchase strategy differs by order type.

Fabrication

A 3D Printer can create a Liberator pistol from two Filaments. The supplied tutorial recommends early Performance upgrades to shorten production time.

Playing tips

  • Orders are not simply a request for any firearm: match the permit class and each numerical stat.
  • For the Gunsmith start, special Class I customers appear on days 1 and 4.
