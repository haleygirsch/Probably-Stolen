Quality, condition, durability and purity affect the price you can obtain for goods.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
Value rangeCondition and purity adjustments can range from -100% to +200%.
DurabilityMachines, weapons and tools can appear in damaged or shabby conditions.
WaterWater uses its own grade computed from the portion that is water.
Chemicals and wineGrades or fermentation scores determine value; aging is a separate wine bonus.

Practical checklist

  • Check condition before a high-value sale.
  • Do not assume an item marked “New” has the same price as one in “Good” condition.
  • Keep bottle price and liquid price distinct.
