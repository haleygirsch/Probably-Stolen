Store requests are specific orders that pay for the listed item requirements within their deadline.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
RequestsReview each item type, quantity and requested quality.
TimeSome requests expire, so check dates before crafting.
RewardsCredits or faction benefits may depend on successful completion.

Practical checklist

  • Set aside already-owned items before accepting more orders.
  • Compare material costs to payout.
  • Avoid selling reserved supplies at the counter.
