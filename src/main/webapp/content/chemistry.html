Lab Pure — demonstration workflow

  1. Put four Chemical Equipment modules into the Chem Finisher. In the demo, two modules introduced 3% contaminants, while four reduced that addition to zero.
  2. Inspect every ingredient and vessel with the Composition Spectrometer. Residue left in a reused bottle can contaminate a new batch.
  3. Blend Dream Caps into mush. The creator’s example used three caps.
  4. Ensure the Heating Plate is OFF before loading the ingredients to avoid accidentally cooking or burning them.
  5. For the demonstrated Dream Dust recipe, add alcohol at about five times the mush amount; the example used 43 ml of mush and approximately 215 ml of alcohol.
  6. Follow the remaining recipe steps and monitor unwanted components. Finish only when the spectrometer indicates sufficiently clean composition.
Grade boundary

Exactly 99.00% does not count as Lab Pure in the supplied walkthrough. The threshold given was 99.01% or higher.
