Modules modify machine performance, efficiency, quality or specialized production functions.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
GeneralPerformance, Efficiency, Quality, Overclock, Eco and Refinement are distinct upgrade types.
HydroponicsHydroreed filtering, Fillerweed output, Nutrifruit output, Kotton auto-loom and Dream Cap functions.
PrinterMetal Printer and Computer Numerical Control enable specialized outputs.
GeneticsGenetic Stabilizer, Positive/Negative Trait Filter and Precision Extraction affect DNA work.
HypertechCapped and Uncapped Neural Core Modules are high-tier, high-legality-risk components.

Practical checklist

  • Choose a module for the bottleneck: speed, power cost, output or quality.
  • Check the installation slot count; an Expansion Kit can add capacity.
  • A capped Hypertech module is High-Level contraband; uncapped is Critical-Level.

How machine module stats combine

Additional game-reference details, rewritten and organized for quick lookup.

MechanicHow it works
PerformanceSpeed = base speed × (1 + total Performance / 100).
EfficiencyPower draw = base draw × (1 − total Efficiency / 100), minimum 1.
QualityChanges the quality of the resulting output according to machine-specific rules.
3D PrinterOnly 25% of the Quality total counts.
DNA ManipulatorPerformance and Quality contributions are halved.
Hydroponic SystemPerformance and Quality contributions are halved.
Mirage ProjectorPerformance ×0.25, Quality ×0.5.
AgeWellPerformance and Efficiency effects are halved.
Module gridA 7×5 grid has 26 usable spaces initially, then 31, then all 35 after upgrades.

Playing tips

  • Install specialized modules for the machine you are actually using, then optimize universal modules for the specific output or power need.
  • Negative Efficiency increases consumption rather than reducing it.
