The routine combines customer trading, inspection, rent planning, machine work and a nighttime trip.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
During shop hoursPrepare inventory, open the counter, inspect buyers or sellers, make an offer, and close when done.
After closingUse station travel, shops and services; return to prepare machinery, electricity and inventory.
At bedtimeNightly production, fees, and events are processed as the day advances.

Practical checklist

  • Reserve rent money before buying expensive modules.
  • Check batteries and inputs on each machine before sleeping.
  • Read the night log for production, wounds and story deliveries.
