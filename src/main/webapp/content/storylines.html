Spoiler-heavy choice guide for recurring visitors. Follow the dates, keep the named items, and pay attention to actions that end a chain. Story-mode dates and some triggers can vary by starting options or patches.

Mode warning

Endless Mode excludes Georgio, Officer Lun, Eleanor and Cadet Serpico storylines. Skip Intro removes several fixed first-two-week scenes, without rescheduling the later fixed story days.

Lou

Wanted Black Market character; bounty 1,500 Credits. Her first encounter becomes available after buying the Business Permit. Reporting her starts a dangerous multi-visit plot.

Before her first visit

Own the Business Permit. Beginning the next morning, she can appear when you have under 75 Credits or cannot pay an upcoming rent or mortgage; otherwise a visit comes by 15 days after the permit. Known Snitch blocks this route.

First encounter

She buys melee weapons, a Plasma Cutter or a Screwdriver. Reporting her earns 1,500 Credits and Security +5. Ignoring her means she does not return. Shooting her does not kill her, but creates an alternate retaliation route.

After reporting: operative

The next day a Republic Operative delivers a Republic Transmitter and offers a further 750 Credits if she is captured.

At least 3 days later: door visit

Lou returns and may ask for weapons, ammunition and medical items worth 500 Credits (or some Credits to cover a shortfall). If the transmitter is visible on your counter, she can notice it. Helping her leads to a shootout report and a later expansion-kit gift.

Two mornings later: planted device

A device appears hidden in a container. This early bomb is a dud, not the final lethal check; interacting with it disarms it.

At least 4 days later: box delivery

Lou leaves a Box of Toys containing Old Republic Orders and the items she bought during the first meeting; if she bought nothing, it includes a Wire Cutter, Plasma Cutter, Tazer and Kitchen Knife.

At least 1 day later: box collection

Return the intact box and every original toy. Missing items or the box can cause a real bomb and game over. Hide the transmitter inside to have her captured; the operative pays 750 Credits the next day. Otherwise, Lou may offer a shabby equipment gift.

At least 4 days later: disguised inspection

If the chain continues, Lou pretends to be Security and demands weapons, ammunition and medicine. Required value: 500 on Price Gouge, 750 on Mark Up, 1,000 on Thin Margins, 1,500 on Red Ink.

At least 7 days later: collection

Put qualifying supplies on the counter. Paying the full demand ends the threat. If at least half is offered, the “That’s All I Have” choice can take the difference in Credits; less than half can trigger the Bombed ending.

Alternative: shooting her

She survives, ceases normal visits and may leave Lou’s Locker. Keep it available and fill it with at least half the required goods, or place the transmitter inside to have her captured. Losing the deadline or failing payment can end the run.

Safety checklist for the game

Do not lose her Box of Toys or any original contents. Keep your transmitter hidden inside the return container rather than openly displayed. Watch the nightly deadline and set aside enough weapon/medical stock before a collection encounter. Exact event spacing is relative to when previous visits occur.

Eleanor

Upper Levels exile, first visits day 23; several choices can end or change her path.

Day 23

She buys a variety of food with a budget of 300 Credits.

Day 25

She buys household goods (200 Credits).

Day 27

She offers spring water, tampons and perfume as a discounted lot.

Day 28

Her supposed uncle delivers a free Care Package. Accepting or reporting the uncle sends the route down different paths.

Day 29 or later

If out of money, she needs food; giving food or 50 Credits counts as helping. Dismissing her ends the storyline.

Care Package trap

Displaying the package at her out-of-money or scavenging visit gets her arrested. Two days later bail is offered: pay 500 Credits, or vouch for her with 40 Security reputation, to continue her story.

Later visits

She scavenges, saves 800 Credits for an investigator, and eventually uncovers a data drive about Nutrifruit research.

Final decision

Return the drive for 300 Credits or leak it to the Lower Levels paper. Two days later she gives a SYNC Self-Refilling Neuroactive Perfume. The leak also changes future Nutrifruit seeds and her faction outcome.

Do not accidentally end this chain

If you want to see her full quest, help her when she has no food, avoid shooting her, and do not decline bail after a Care Package arrest.

Cadet Serpico

Security cadet whose later story involves corruption, a wrongful warrant and a food rescue.

Day 40

Buys food and treats (100 Credits).

Day 42

Troubled by Security corruption, buys alcohol (120 Credits).

Day 43

Has exposed corruption; buys food, drinks and treats, but no alcohol.

Day 45

Security claims he is wanted and asks you to report him.

Day 46

Serpico tells you he was framed. Reporting or shooting him pays 300 Credits but ends the chain.

Day 48

Georgio brings a letter. Leave 2,000 kcal of food at the Dumping Ground within three nights, or pay Georgio 150 Credits to deliver it (free at 40 Lower Levels reputation).

Days 48–50

Place the food on the Dumping Ground grid when leaving. Partial delivery extends his deadline by a night each time.

Day 51

If under 2,000 kcal, the paper announces his death and the story ends.

Day 52

After a successful rescue, Security increases the warrant reward to 600 Credits.

Day 54

Choose where to send his evidence: Revolution, Black Market, or Security (requires 50 Security reputation).

Day 57

Receive +12 reputation with your chosen faction and choose a shabby Storage Bay (Extended), four Module Bay Expansion Kits or two Current Converters. Revolution/Black Market choices can leave him wanted again.

Food planning

It is the sum of calories multiplied by quantities that matters—not the sale value of the food. Do not accidentally sell the supplies you set aside.

Officer Lun

Security officer. His introductory duties and his later addiction story are separate.

Day 1

Explains the shop alarm and provides the Security Wanted List (without Skip Intro).

Day 9

Explains additional reporting expectations.

Day 11

Buys food; optional.

Day 12

Wounded. Narcotics raise his addiction; ordinary medicine does not open the later addicted branch.

Day 13

Report the specific Lower Levels seller who ambushed Lun, not James Bailey. Bounty: 150 Credits.

Day 16

A second chance to sell a narcotic. Synaprest instead reduces addiction by 10 per injector.

Day 20

Only if addicted: he sells stolen armory equipment. Reporting him pays 100 Credits and ends the chain.

Day 24

Only if still addicted and free: fake narcotics inspection. Call Security for 200 Credits and reputation; otherwise he confiscates narcotics.

Creator-tested bounty path

Day-13 ambusher 150 + day-24 Lun 200 = 350 Credits in bounties. The earlier day-20 report yields 250 in combined bounties instead.

Georgio

Lower Levels scavenger, scheduled from day 6 to day 43, with barter deals, Liquid Luck and a repayable loan.

Day 6

Offers “alien relics” (actually Scrap Metal) at a discount.

Day 9

Offers inexpensive large water bottles.

Day 12

Sells a discounted Kid’s Backpack (Large).

Day 15

Offers three nights of Dumping Ground help for 50 Credits.

Day 18

Sells fake Hemingway cigarettes. Inspection can reveal counterfeits.

Day 22

Stolen toiletries; hidden wanted report opportunity (100 Credits).

Day 27

Gives one Liquid Luck.

Day 32

Offers three Liquid Luck at a 20% discount.

Day 37

Sells a stolen evidence locker, with another hidden-wanted report opportunity.

Day 43

Asks for a loan of one quarter or one half of your cash. If lent, he eventually pays four times the loan, with an earlier return on an unaffordable rent day or a normal return from day 69.

Loan risk

Do not lend cash that you immediately need for rent. His repaid amount is large, but the payout may not arrive before your next obligations.

Li Bei Wen

Wanted researcher, 400-Credit reward, with an alternative route to his non-addictive painkiller formula.

First visit (day 14 earliest, day 5 with Skip Intro)

Offers eight Nomore Injector samples at 50% off. Reporting or shooting him ends the story for a 400-Credit reward.

Three days later

Wants Common and Restricted Chemical Supplies. Selling each Common supply reduces his wait by four days; each Restricted supply reduces it by seven.

Final visit

Gives the Nomore Formula (listed as 400 Credits) and two high-purity Nomore Injectors. Base wait is 17 days, reducible to two days.

For the research ending, do not report him during the first or second meeting, and consider selling him chemical supplies to shorten the timeline.

Sources / further detail: Li Bei Wen ↗

Joe Wild

Insurance and access to the Lower Levels contact network.

Day 4

Introduces theft insurance, which can be switched before sleeping.

Day 7

Opens the network. Starting favor depends on difficulty: 300 / 200 / 100 / 0 from Price Gouge through Red Ink.

Later

Phone 8376 lets you access his services. He may return with reminders.

Once you own the store

He can offer a referral to a Hypertech Smuggler for a fee.

Sources / further detail: Joe Wild ↗

Sawyer

Janitorial Staff contact and Underground Exchange service.

Day 10

Janitorial service fee introduced in normal story mode.

Day 25

Underground Exchange unlocks: deposit requested items in the trash can, use the janitorial service and check the next morning for the reward.

With Sawyer’s Crew

Exchange access can begin day 1.

Critical consequence

Killing Sawyer ends both the janitorial service and the exchange, and causes additional Black Market reputation loss.

Odin Haraldsson

Wanted Revolution buyer, 300-Credit bounty, normally on day 10 (day 2 when Skip Intro is enabled).

First visit

Decide whether to report him or keep him available as a buyer/contact.

Repeatable access

The storyline includes a phone contact; see the original linked page for the latest call conditions and possible stock.

He is useful as a Revolution-side customer; reporting for the bounty closes that opportunity.

John Smith

Wanted customer with a 125-Credit reward; asks for everyday grocery goods.

Encounter

Check the Wanted List and match his identity before deciding whether to sell or report.

Report

Collecting the bounty ends his visit; the ordinary purchase route preserves the sale instead.
