Storage bays expand inventory capacity and provide protected or specialized storage.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
RegularStorage Bay, Extended Storage Bay and Machine Bay are expansion tiers.
SpecialistGunsmith and Chemist Storage Bays suit their production areas.
SmugglerMini and full Smuggler Bays are distinct secure storage classes.
HypertechHypertech Storage Bay is a late-game storage option listed at 10,000 Credits.

Practical checklist

  • Keep quest boxes in safe storage only if the specific storyline allows it.
  • Do not remove or lose Lou’s original Box of Toys.
  • Choose expansions for the type of inventory filling your shop.

Playing tips

  • Store rare quest items in a place where you can find them before their deadlines.
  • Machine Bays and Smuggler’s Bays obey different restrictions; don’t assume a backpack fits every bay.
