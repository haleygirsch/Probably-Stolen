The Dumping Ground offers scavenging after hours, with loot and wound risks.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
AttemptsUp to 5 searches per night.
Category oddsJunk 65%; Materials 15%; Medical 5% on a normal day.
Fresh DumpWednesdays; higher share of valuable materials than regular days.
First wound risk5% on the first search, before Luck and other modifiers.
SerpicoThe Ground grid is where food for his rescue is delivered.

Practical checklist

  • Bring Screwdriver, Wire Cutter or Plasma Cutter for some extra resource finds.
  • Avoid scavenging while badly wounded if you need uninterrupted shop days.
  • Check the Ground grid to confirm food placement, not the counter.
