Key mechanics

TopicDetails
Normal retail markup+15% on Mark Up difficulty, if the customer accepts retail markup.
Cozy retail markup+20%; hard and challenge +0%.
Contraband markupLow +35%, Mid +70%, High +100%, Critical +150%.
Tips and tourist tax+15% each where their conditions are met.
RoundingThe game rounds to whole Credits; halfway values go to the even integer.

Practical checklist

  • Inspect condition and purity before pricing expensive items.
  • Add bottle/container values separately from the contents.
  • Perform bargains after accounting for automatic modifiers.

Playing tips

  • Item containers and their contents are accounted for separately before final deal modifiers.
  • The price visible to a specific buyer can differ from the reference base price, even when the item is identical.
