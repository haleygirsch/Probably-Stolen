Key mechanics

TopicDetails

WaterWater Purifier and Moisture Farm process or make water.
PowerEnergy cells fit battery slots, while power-service machines and boosters have separate requirements.

Practical checklist

  • Choose the machine for the product you want, then reserve enough energy.
  • Buy specialist modules only after the needed machine is online.
  • Use Dr. Jackson’s nightly stock to find equipment.
