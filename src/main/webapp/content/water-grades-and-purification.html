Early Water Merchant strategy

  1. Take Working Pipes when using this starting scenario if available.
  2. Convert starting toxic water to at least base grade using the starting purifier consumable.
  3. Save most early Credits for three Water Purifiers, batteries and rent.
  4. Place a standard bottle of rust water into each powered purifier nightly.
  5. As machines come online, switch your selling inventory to High-Quality bottles.
Useful distinction

High-Quality water is enough for many sales and wine recipes. Pure water is a separate achievement requiring sufficiently high machine quality.

Playing tips

  • Check a container using AquaScan; “99% clean” is not the same as zero minerals, and some plants require minerals.
  • Dilution changes the concentration of each contaminant and nutrient, not only the grade name.
