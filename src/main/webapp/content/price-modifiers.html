The market and each customer’s perks add price modifiers before final bargaining.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
SellingTourist Tax +15%, Variety +15%, Vending Machine +10%; perfume +15% or +25%.
BuyingGood Inspection Discount -15%; Five Finger Discount on stolen goods ranges from -10% to -25%.
Negotiation penaltiesUntreated addictions -8% each when selling, +8% each when buying.
Retail markup+20% Price Gouge, +15% Mark Up, 0% on harder difficulties.
Final dealBargains are applied as a separate multiplicative step.

Practical checklist

  • Compare the trade tablet’s breakdown to the item condition before accepting.
  • Watch market events and difficulty-dependent vendor markups.
  • Use careful authenticity inspection to earn an inspection discount.
