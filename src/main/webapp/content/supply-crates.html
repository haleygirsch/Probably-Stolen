Crates are sealed stocks with category-specific loot and chance-based content.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
SourcesSpecial scavengers, departments and the Commissary can supply different crate types.
AccessSome crates require the correct access card to open.
LuckPositive Luck can improve contents by multiplying eligible rolls.
SecuritySecurity crates and evidence lockers can have contraband implications.

Practical checklist

  • Verify which crate you have before using a rare access card.
  • Avoid comparing an unopened crate to the value of a guaranteed item.
  • Use the Luck tab before opening chance-based loot.
