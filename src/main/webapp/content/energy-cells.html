Battery slots power machines in kJ, with several normal and specialized cell types.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
Energy Cell100 kJ when full; sells for 110 Credits.
Mk. II250 kJ when full; listed full value 280 Credits.
Self Charging100 kJ capacity, gains 4 kJ each night.
Disposable Battery1,000 kJ, not rechargeable; Low-Level contraband.
Hypertech Power CellPowers devices up to 12 kJ output, increased by 10 for each fitted Hypertech Electronic Parts.
Recharger / PowerBlocRestore 3 kJ / 2 kJ per night before upgrades.

Practical checklist

  • Put a charged cell in every powered machine before sleeping.
  • Power service can be interrupted by blackouts.
  • Current Converter pays power costs directly in Credits.

Machine battery and charging reference

Additional game-reference details, rewritten and organized for quick lookup.

MechanicHow it works
Energy Cell100 kJ when full; 110 Credits including stored charge.
Energy Cell Mk. II250 kJ capacity; 280 Credits when full.
Self Charging Energy Cell100 kJ maximum and regenerates 4 kJ nightly.
Disposable Battery1,000 kJ; cannot be recharged and is Low-Level Contraband.
Hypertech Power CellProvides up to 12 kJ of machine output, plus 10 for each Hypertech electronic part attached.
RechargerAdds 3 kJ per night to each eligible inserted battery (6 with Commercial Power).
PowerBlocAdds 2 kJ per night to adjacent eligible cells (4 with Commercial Power).
Current ConverterCan pay machine power expenses in Credits rather than consuming a normal battery.

Playing tips

  • Recharger and PowerBloc need paid Power Service, and blackouts suspend their ordinary charging.
  • Electric Eels can continue charging batteries during a blackout.
