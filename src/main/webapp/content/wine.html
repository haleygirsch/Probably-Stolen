Reliable Master Vintage checklist

  1. Inspect each BloomBerry with the Magnifying Glass; do not use a moldy berry.
  2. Use one small berry and one normal berry as the demonstrated easy combination.
  3. Use High-Quality water (Pure is not required for the same water score).
  4. Add yeast to gain a score point and speed fermentation.
  5. Make sure the total ingredient score reaches 6 or more before pressing Start Fermenting.
  6. Let the finished bottle age for extra value, optionally in the AgeWell.
