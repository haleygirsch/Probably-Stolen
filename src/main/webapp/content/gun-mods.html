Weapon attachments affect gun statistics and whether an order’s requirements are fulfilled.

Key mechanics

TopicDetails
SlotsCompatible weapons accept a limited set of modifications.
ExamplesMagazines, optics, suppressors, grips, stocks, internal springs and underbarrel attachments.
OrdersThe customer checks the required firearm class and requested statistics.
PermitWeapon permits can reduce restricted-market price additions.

Practical checklist

  • Read a buyer’s requested stat lines before applying modifications.
  • Check whether a mod fits the exact firearm model.
  • Compare a modification’s value to the order reward.
