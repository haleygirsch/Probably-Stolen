Recommended farming loop

  1. Unlock Aquaculture and then Aquaculture II to access Sturgeon and the eels through the retired aqua farmer.
  2. Keep Gutter Tilapia, which tolerate Gutterflow Pellets, as a low-cost source of fish fillets.
  3. Use those fillets as meat feed for the adult Sturgeon.
  4. When a Sturgeon becomes gravid, harvest caviar with a Surgery Toolset if you wish to keep it alive.
  5. Allow Sturgeon health to recover above 90% before expecting fresh eggs.
